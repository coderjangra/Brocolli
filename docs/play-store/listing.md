**Full description** (≤4000):

```
Broke College Living, sorted.

Brocolli is the fastest way for a friend group to split expenses: chai, cabs, Zomato, rent, trips. Log it the moment you pay. Settle up on UPI without the awkward "bro, you owe me" texts.

CATCHES YOUR PAYMENTS
Pay on any UPI app and Brocolli turns the bank alert into a card in your Inbox. Swipe right to split it, left if it was just you, up to skip. No typing amounts, no forgetting.
• Works with bank SMS alerts and payment app notifications
• Two bank accounts? Brocolli tracks each one and skips money you move between them
• Mute payees you never split (rent autopay, recharges)

SPLIT ANY WAY
• Equal, exact amounts, percentages or shares
• Multiple payers, groups and 1-to-1 ledgers
• Quick add: type "250 zomato w rahul aman" and you're done
• Every paisa adds up: no rounding errors, ever

ONE NUMBER PER FRIEND
See exactly who owes whom, and tap any balance to see why. Simplify group debts into the fewest payments.

SETTLE ON UPI IN ONE TAP
Opens your UPI app with the amount filled in. UPI app being difficult? Show a QR code or copy the UPI ID. Send a payment reminder over WhatsApp.

YOUR MONEY, YOUR PHONE
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking.
• Brocolli never holds or moves your money.
• Only payment messages are read. Everything else is ignored on your phone and never stored.
• Export to CSV anytime.

See where your money goes each month, by account, and meet Broc, the broccoli who's happiest when everyone's square.

For users 18+.
```
